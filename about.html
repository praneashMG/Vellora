How It Began

From a Small Studio to a Beloved Brand

Elegant Invites started in 2014 in a tiny corner studio with a vintage letterpress, a stack of cotton paper, and a belief that wedding stationery should feel as meaningful as the vows it announces.

What began as a one-woman operation has grown into a small, dedicated team of designers, calligraphers, and printmakers — but our philosophy hasn't changed. We still treat every suite as if it were our own, obsessing over paper weight, ink coverage, and the way an envelope feels when a guest opens it.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Three principles guide every decision we make — from the papers we choose to the way we communicate with our clients.

Craftsmanship First

We use traditional printing methods — letterpress, foil stamping, hand calligraphy — because they create results that digital printing simply cannot replicate.

Real Human Service

No chatbots, no automated responses. When you email us, you're talking to the same designer who will work on your suite from start to finish.

Thoughtful Sustainability

We source recycled and responsibly forested papers, use soy-based inks, and minimize waste in every step of our production process.
